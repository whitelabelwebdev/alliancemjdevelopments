The company

An Edmonton company specializing in the commercial development and management of neighbourhood and community retail centres, built around the success of our retail partners and to be a destination of choice for consumers.

For over 20 years we have planned, developed, sold and leased millions of square feet of prime real estate in Edmonton and beyond, with millions more planned and in development.
