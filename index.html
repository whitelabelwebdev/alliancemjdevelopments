The company

Neighbourhood centres built to be the destination of choice.

Alliance MJ Developments is an Edmonton company specializing in the commercial development and management of neighbourhood and community retail centres.

For over 20 years we have planned, developed, sold and leased millions of square feet of prime real estate in Edmonton and beyond, with millions more planned and in development. Everything we build comes back to two commitments: the success of our retail partners, and making each property a place consumers choose to visit.
